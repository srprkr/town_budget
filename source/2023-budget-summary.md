## Notes

- Revenue total checks out exactly against the PDF-stated $8,426,376 (verified by summing all 15 fund values; off by $1 rounding).
- Expenditure total: manual sum yields $9,035,054; the PDF-stated total was approximately $9,047,513. Discrepancy of ~$12,459 is within estimation error (see below).
- PDF is a scanned image document; all values extracted via visual reading of high-resolution (300 DPI) page images.
- Revenue values are confirmed directly from the 2023 Budget "Summary by Fund" page.
- Expenditure values are derived from the Increase/(Decrease) column (arithmetic from 2022 base) and cross-referenced with the 2024 Budget Summary by Fund page. Some values are estimates.
- Ambulance Service Fund (10) appears here for the first time (not present in 2022 budget).
- Sewer Fund (08): expenditure of $820,615 vs revenue of $479,400 — $341,215 deficit, consistent with ongoing capital/operational shortfall.
- Capital Projects Fund (31): $1,136,180 revenue vs $1,393,780 expenditure — draws on reserves. Substantially smaller than 2022 ($2.25M) and 2024 ($3.47M).
- Street Tax Fund (18): Revenue dropped sharply from $147,012 (2022) to $75,433 (2023) — likely reflects reduced state allocation or timing.
- State Liquid Fuels Fund (35): Expenditure of $263,652 vs revenue of $117,269 — draws heavily on reserves or prior balances.
- 2022 Budget columns in the 2023 PDF differ slightly from the original 2022 Budget document due to budget amendments adopted after the 2022 budget was originally passed.
- The same Summary by Fund page also shows 2022 Year-End Projections: total revenue $8,096,352, total expenditure $7,143,963.
