## Notes

- Revenue total checks out exactly against the PDF-stated $9,553,110 (verified by summing all 14 fund values).
- Expenditure total: manual sum yields $9,149,333 vs. PDF-stated $9,149,332 (difference of $1, rounding).
- Ambulance Service Fund (10) is not present in the 2022 budget; it appears for the first time in 2024.
- PDF is a scanned image document (Canon scanner); no embedded text. All values extracted via visual reading of page images.
- Police Pension Fund (60) revenue of $428,121 is notably higher than Fire Protection, Library, and other pension funds due to state aid and investment earnings.
- Capital Projects Fund (31) is substantially smaller than 2024 ($2.25M vs. $3.47M revenue), reflecting fewer large infrastructure projects budgeted that year.
- Sewer Fund (08): $501,400 in / $634,099 out — $132,699 deficit, consistent with operational shortfalls seen in other years.
