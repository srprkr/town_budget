## Notes

- General Fund revenue ($3,024,028) is notably lower than 2019 ($4,203,683). The budget narrative attributes this to a decrease in Business Privilege Tax (Act 511) revenues, following a prior-year overpayment issue requiring refunds, and a Borough policy of prudent cash reserve utilization.
- General Fund stated goal: no millage increase for 2018 (total millage held at 7.753 mills).
- Sewer Fund revenue ($1,740,026) is inflated by **Proceeds from Long-Term Debt** (~$1.24M) for sanitary sewer rehabilitation construction — operating sewer revenue was ~$500K.
- Capital Projects Fund ($1,208,811 in / $1,519,613 out) funded by: Transportation grants ($179,784), Sewer Rehabilitation grant ($361,218), HUD/CDBG grants ($160,000), EOC Simulator grant ($400,000), ADA/CDBG-I grants ($100,000), and a small GF transfer ($6,609). Expenditures include a WWI Monument project ($67,337) and Public Art ($10,000).
- Fire Apparatus Fund (04): reserve fund — revenue from real estate tax millage accumulates for future apparatus purchases; $0 budgeted expenditure in 2018.
- Street Tax Fund runs a large planned deficit ($75,497 in / $271,214 out), drawing down reserves for road construction ($150,000 capital).
- Police Pension Fund expenditure ($360,197) exceeds revenue ($333,349); pension payments reflect an active force with growing retirement obligations.
- PDF is a 68-page scanned document; fund totals read from the individual fund narrative pages (Appendix D–Q), cross-checked against the real estate tax millage table.
