# 2016 Borough Budget Summary

**Source:** https://jenkintownboro.com/2016-budget/
**Adopted:** December 14, 2015

## Overview

Total operating budget: **$6.5 million**. The General Fund comprises 56.11% of the budget. No tax millage increase for the General Fund; a 0.15-mill increase was approved for the Fire Apparatus Fund (from 0.30 to 0.45 mills).

## Notes

- No full budget PDF is publicly available for 2016. The borough website links back to this narrative page only.
- The $2.1M property tax total represents roughly 32% of the $6.5M total budget; remaining revenue comes from Act 511 taxes, parking, grants, sewer rents, etc. — none of which are itemized on the page.
- The Fire Apparatus Fund projected a year-end 2015 balance of $539,227. The fund was established in 2011 to save for fire truck replacement (first replacement projected 2019, estimated $1–1.2M).
- No drilldown line items are available. The donut chart for 2016 shows property tax allocation by fund only.
