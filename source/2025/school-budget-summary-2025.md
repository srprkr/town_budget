## 2024-25 Budget Highlights

- **Total budget:** $19,326,403 (balanced; 5.53% tax increase required)
- **Tax millage:** 50.300 mills (up from 47.665 in 2023-24)
- **Average RE tax bill:** $7,537 (up $395 from $7,142)
- **PSERS employer retirement rate:** 33.99%
- **State Property Tax Reduction Allocation:** $800,812 total (~$740 credit per homestead)

## Revenue Notes

- **Local revenue** is 78% of total; state 21%; federal 1%.
- **Assessed values** dropped to $276,753,068 in 2024-25 (from $284,156,099 in 2023-24), a loss of $7,403,031 in assessed value equating to ~$374,379 in lost tax revenue.
- **State basic education funding** increase of $296,298 proposed; only half included pending final state budget.
- **ESSER funding** ends — loss of ~$238,000 in federal revenue vs. prior year.
