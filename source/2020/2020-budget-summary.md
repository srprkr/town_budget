## Notes

- Revenue total verified by summing all 14 fund values: $8,572,080.
- Debt Service Fund revenue corrected from initial misread of $260,549 to $250,549 (verified against crop image of PDF page 57).
- PDF is a scanned image document (72 pages, landscape budget tables printed portrait); all values extracted via Ghostscript-rendered PNG crops rotated 90°, reading the "2020 Budget" column (8th of 10 columns at x≈2090–2420 in the 3300px-wide rotated image).
- Sewer Fund: $435,300 in / $964,867 out — large deficit driven by $415,000 in Sewer Construction capital expenditure.
- Capital Projects Fund: $1,920,243 in / $2,157,205 out — funded primarily by State & Federal Grants ($1,879,243), covering Sewer Rehabilitation, DCED/DCNR/FEMA grants, CDBG HC Ramps, and Transportation projects.
- Fire Apparatus Fund revenue ($128,764) is unusually high relative to expenditure ($34,466) due to real property tax levy of $126,764 building reserves for future apparatus purchases.
- General Fund runs a deficit: $4,149,596 in / $4,220,230 out.
