## Notes

- General Fund revenue ($3,535,485) is lower than both 2016 and 2019 due to a drop in Business Privilege Tax (Act 511) revenues — BPT dropped from $1,125,000 (2016 budget) to $975,000 (2017), following refund/overpayment issues with third-party tax collectors. Borough hired a specialist firm to address this.
- General Fund budgeted ending balance: $459,633 (13.0% of revenues), funded by drawing down beginning balance of $783,610.
- Street Lighting Fund runs a large deficit ($57,672 in / $76,400 out) due to Borough-wide LED streetlight upgrades; maintenance expenditures are temporarily elevated.
- Fire Apparatus Fund (04): pure reserve fund — $127,946 accumulates from real estate millage; $0 expenditure. Projected reserve total by end of 2017: $797,081 (next major apparatus purchase projected 2019 at ~$1.2M).
- Library Fund small deficit ($233,200 in / $234,823 out) — contribution plus $5,000 reserve to balance.
- Sewer Fund deficit ($449,500 in / $566,577 out) — primarily Sewer Construction spending ($222,327) for PA DEP-required conveyance system rehabilitation. Fund balance used to cover shortfall.
- Capital Projects Fund ($859,828 in / $971,074 out) — funded by grants: Transportation ($179,784), Sewer Rehabilitation ($381,518), CDBG HC Ramps ($160,000), Playground ($20,000), EOC Generator ($40,000), and transfers from GF ($10,000) and Sewer Fund ($67,327). Expenditures match grants plus Borough match/engineering.
- Highway/Liquid Fuels Fund: 2017 includes new truck dump-body replacement ($30,000) and road paving project cost-shared with PECO (Borough share: ~$19,650). Debt service on 2015 GON Loan: principal $24,317 + interest $5,717.
- Police Pension: significant deficit ($301,756 in / $360,197 out) — pension payments ($325,197) exceed combined contributions and state aid; fund draws on accumulated assets. Pension fund balance projected at $4,080,887.
- Non-Uniformed Pension: large surplus ($86,570 in / $30,831 out) — contributions and state aid far exceed current pension payments, growing the fund balance. Fund balance projected at $599,490.
- Fund numbering differs slightly from later years: Street Tax = Fund 18, Debt Service = Fund 20, Police Donations = Fund 95.
