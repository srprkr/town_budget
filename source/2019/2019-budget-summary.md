## Notes

- General Fund is nearly balanced ($4,203,683 in / $4,203,115 out) — a significant improvement over 2018's ~$977K structural deficit. The 0.4-mill tax increase restored revenue to a sustainable level.
- Fire Apparatus Fund revenue ($813,750) is inflated by **Proceeds from Long-Term Debt** (~$786,481) for a new apparatus purchase — operating revenue (real estate tax) was ~$27,269. Expenditure ($757,806) reflects the capital purchase and debt interest.
- Capital Projects Fund ($1,738,338 in / $2,019,248 out) funded by state and federal grants covering sewer rehabilitation, transportation, CDBG, and other infrastructure grants. Borough match draws down reserves.
- Sewer Fund runs a small deficit ($497,000 in / $547,787 out), with the gap covered by fund balance; operating expenditures include treatment fees, debt service, and maintenance.
- Street Tax Fund deficit ($116,340 in / $169,668 out) reflects continued road construction draw-down of reserves.
- State Liquid Fuels Fund deficit ($122,159 in / $155,034 out) reflects higher road maintenance and debt service spending than state allocation.
- Non-Uniformed Pension Fund surplus ($101,665 in / $31,681 out) reflects employer/employee contributions and state aid exceeding benefit payments — growing fund balance.
